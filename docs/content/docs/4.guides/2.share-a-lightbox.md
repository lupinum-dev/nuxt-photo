---
title: Share a lightbox
description: Give several albums one explicit collection and navigation order.
---

Wrap related recipes in `PhotoGroup` and pass the complete ordered collection once.

```vue
<script setup lang="ts">
const allPhotos = computed(() => [...landscapes, ...portraits])
</script>

<template>
  <PhotoGroup :photos="allPhotos">
    <section>
      <h2>Landscapes</h2>
      <PhotoAlbum :photos="landscapes" layout="rows" />
    </section>
    <section>
      <h2>Portraits</h2>
      <PhotoAlbum :photos="portraits" layout="columns" />
    </section>
  </PhotoGroup>
</template>
```

::collection-ownership-lab
::

## Important decision

`allPhotos` owns next and previous order. The album sections can move visually without changing that order.

## Failure modes

- A descendant ID missing from `PhotoGroup.photos` throws.
- Duplicate IDs are invalid.
- A group mounted with `lightbox=false` remains inert. Remount it to change setup-time capabilities.

## Verify

Open the last photo in the first album and navigate next. The first photo from the second album should appear.
