---
title: Build a lightbox from primitives
description: Compose a custom lightbox structure while keeping Nuxt Photo state, gestures, and transitions.
---

Use primitives when the built-in `<Lightbox>` structure cannot match your
design. You own the markup and final accessibility review. Nuxt Photo keeps the
photo state, navigation, gestures, zoom, focus trap, and opening transition.

If you only need different captions or action buttons, use
[the built-in lightbox slots](/docs/guides/customize-the-built-in-lightbox) instead.

## Compose the provider and triggers

`LightboxProvider` owns one photo collection. Each `PhotoTrigger` connects a
thumbnail to its matching photo. The lower-level components inside
`LightboxRoot` draw the backdrop, the slides, the controls, and the caption.

## Frame the photo and place the caption

The lightbox fits each photo inside a frame area: the full-screen slide minus a
mat on each side. Set the mat with three CSS variables on `LightboxRoot`. The
frame area is measured inside `LightboxViewport`, so give the viewport a
position such as `relative` or `absolute`.

| Variable                  | Default                   | Space it keeps free   |
| ------------------------- | ------------------------- | --------------------- |
| `--np-frame-inset-top`    | `clamp(40px, 7vh, 80px)`  | Above the photo.      |
| `--np-frame-inset-bottom` | `clamp(40px, 7vh, 80px)`  | Below the photo.      |
| `--np-frame-inset-inline` | `clamp(48px, 6vw, 120px)` | Left and right of it. |

The defaults need the structure CSS. Without it, the photo uses the whole
viewport.

The lightbox then sets where the photo is drawn on `LightboxRoot`, in viewport
pixels: `--np-frame-x`, `--np-frame-y`, `--np-frame-width`, and
`--np-frame-height`. The viewer above uses them to put its caption directly
under the photo.

Keep full-size control layers click-through with `pointer-events: none`, and
turn pointer events back on for the buttons inside. A click that reaches the
viewport beside the photo closes the lightbox.

## Verify the custom structure

Check the behavior that custom markup can affect:

1. Open every thumbnail with click, Enter, and Space.
2. Move through photos with the arrow, Home, and End keys.
3. Close with Escape and confirm focus moves to the thumbnail of the photo you
   were viewing.
4. Confirm the dialog has an accessible name.
5. Test touch swipe, zoom, and reduced motion.
6. Test the layout in left-to-right and right-to-left documents.

Use [the primitives reference](/docs/reference/primitives) for exact props and slot
contracts.
