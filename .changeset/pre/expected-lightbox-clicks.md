Make lightbox clicks do what people expect. A click or tap beside the photo now closes the lightbox. A mouse click on the photo zooms to its real pixels and back, while a touch tap still shows or hides the controls. Zoom and pan now measure the photo as it is drawn inside the lightbox mat, so a zoomed photo stops at its own edge. Hidden controls leave the Tab order and return on mouse movement or Tab. Closing focuses the thumbnail of the photo you were viewing.
